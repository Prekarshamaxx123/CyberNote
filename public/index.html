Seamless Offline Experience

CyberNote keeps a persistent high-performance cache in your browser LocalStorage and SQLite database. When you lose internet connection, you can continue typing, creating notes, drawing diagrams, and reorganizing branches with zero interruptions. The moment your internet reconnects, all pending changes auto-sync to Google Drive and the server.

Ultra-Lightweight Storage Architecture

To protect your 15 GB Google Drive quota, CyberNote compresses uploaded screenshots and canvas drawings into optimized lightweight formats and minifies note metadata so your entire notebook consumes under 0.001% of your storage.
