Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Introduction & Overview

CyberNote ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an open-source, client-first, hierarchical note-taking application designed to maximize user privacy and security. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application accesses, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use CyberNote, specifically regarding your Google user data and cloud backup synchronization.

🛡️ Zero Tracking Guarantee: CyberNote does not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your notes or personal data to advertising networks or third-party data brokers. All note data is stored locally in your browser/device database, and optional cloud backups are synced exclusively to your personal Google Drive account.

2. Information We Access & Collect

CyberNote operates primarily client-side. We only access data necessary to provide seamless note syncing and backup services:

  • Google Account Information: When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (your email address, name, and profile avatar URL) strictly to authenticate you and display your active session status.
  • Google Drive Data (Restricted Scope): We request access to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This restricted permission only allows CyberNote to access, create, update, and read files that were specifically created by CyberNote (such as your encrypted backup file CyberNote_Sync_Tree.json). CyberNote cannot read, view, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Local Storage & IndexedDB / SQLite: Your notes, hierarchy tree, tags, and formatting are saved locally on your device to enable instant offline access and rapid delta-syncing.

3. How We Use Google User Data

We use your Google account information and Drive permissions solely for the following explicit features:

  • To authenticate your identity across sessions and devices.
  • To automatically create, save, and restore encrypted backups of your hierarchical notebook to your personal Google Drive account.
  • To synchronize note modifications across multiple devices running CyberNote.

4. Google API Limited Use Requirements

CyberNote's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not transfer your Google user data to any external servers, third-party analytics services, or artificial intelligence models for training without explicit user consent.
  • We do not use your Google user data for serving advertisements.
  • Human access to user data is strictly prohibited; no team member or administrator has access to your private notes or credentials.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We employ industry-standard cryptographic techniques to safeguard your notes:

  • Client-Side Encryption: Optional password protection utilizes client-side AES-GCM 256-bit encryption. Encrypted notes remain decipherable only on your device using your private passphrase.
  • HTTPS/TLS: All communications between your client and Google APIs are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • Ephemeral Tokens: Google OAuth tokens are stored safely in local session storage and refreshed automatically without exposing user passwords.

6. Data Retention, Deletion & Revocation

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can clear all local notes and settings at any time via the CyberNote settings panel or by clearing your browser storage.
  • Google Drive Backup Deletion: You can delete your CyberNote_Sync_Tree.json backup directly within Google Drive or via the in-app backup management tools.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke CyberNote's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions. Once revoked, CyberNote will lose all access to your Google account and Drive.

7. Children's Privacy

CyberNote does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us to take appropriate steps.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or Google API policy guidelines. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
