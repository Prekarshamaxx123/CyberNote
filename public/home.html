About CyberNote & Application Purpose

CyberNote was created to give students, developers, security researchers, and privacy-conscious writers an intuitive, distraction-free environment to structure complex knowledge without relying on proprietary, locked-in third-party servers.

Unlike traditional note apps that store your personal information on centralized corporate databases, CyberNote functions as a completely autonomous client-side application. All notes, folders, drawings, and passwords stay on your personal device's local encrypted storage (IndexedDB). You have full sovereignty over your information.

🌳 Knowledge Tree Organization

Organize nested folders, sub-topics, research papers, and code snippets in an infinite hierarchy with custom node colors and vector icons.

⚡ 100% Offline Capability

Engineered as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Full access to all your notes and tools on airplane mode or without an active internet connection.

🔐 Local Zero-Knowledge Security

Optional client-side AES-256-GCM encryption with password-derived keys ensures only you can decrypt sensitive personal notes.

Optional Cloud Backup Integration

Why Does CyberNote Integrate with Google Drive?

By default, all your notes live securely in your browser's local cache. However, if you clear your browser cache, change computers, or lose your phone, locally stored data could be lost. To prevent data loss, CyberNote offers an optional, user-initiated Google Drive Cloud Backup feature.

When you choose to sign in with Google, CyberNote automatically synchronizes your notes directly with your personal Google Drive storage. This ensures your knowledge is safely backed up and accessible across all your devices.

Strictly Restricted Permissions (drive.file) CyberNote only requests access to create, edit, and read files that CyberNote itself creates (inside a dedicated CyberNote/ folder). CyberNote CANNOT see or access any other files in your Google Drive.
No Middleman Server Your notes are synced directly between your web browser and official Google API servers over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3). We operate no external databases.
Zero Monetization & No Ads Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for marketing, or used to train artificial intelligence models. Your data belongs exclusively to you.
