Privacy Policy for CyberNote

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

CyberNote ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the CyberNote web application hosted at https://prekarshamaxx123.github.io/CyberNote/.

1. Information We Collect

CyberNote is a client-side note-taking application. We do not operate external database servers that collect, store, or sell your personal data.

  • Local Storage: Your notes, folders, and application settings are stored locally on your device within your web browser's storage (LocalStorage and IndexedDB).
  • Google Account Information: If you choose to connect your Google Account, we receive your basic profile information (name, email address, profile picture) to personalize your workspace header.

2. Google Drive Access and Data Usage

CyberNote integrates with the Google Drive API to provide cloud synchronization for your notes. We request the following permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: This permission allows CyberNote to view, create, edit, and delete only the specific files and folders created by CyberNote (such as the CyberNote folder and your individual note files). CyberNote does not access, read, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

CyberNote's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your notes or personal data with any third parties or advertisers. All synchronization occurs directly between your web browser and Google's official servers via secure HTTPS connections.

5. Data Security

Your notes can be protected with local client-side password encryption (SHA-256). All communications with Google APIs use industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/Prekarshamaxx123/CyberNote.
